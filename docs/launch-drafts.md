## LinkedIn
I've been building PromptPilot AI, a free prompt builder made for how people in India actually work with AI.

Most of us type a one-line request and get a generic answer. PromptPilot turns your goal (in English or Hinglish) into a structured prompt with context, format and constraints, for any model: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Cursor.

What's inside:
- Work-safe, tested templates that tell the AI never to invent facts, numbers or salary data
- Prompt packs for job hunting (CTC in LPA, notice periods), students (boards, JEE, NEET), small shops (WhatsApp offers, prices in ₹) and freelancers (proposals, payment reminders)
- Hindi / Hinglish output option
- Smart mode for an AI rewrite, with a built-in fallback
- A free "Improve my prompt" Chrome extension

It's free and needs no signup. I'd love feedback: https://promptpilot-ai-psi.vercel.app
