# PromptPilot AI: India festival layer research (Oct 2026 to Mar 2027)

Researched and verified on 18 Oct 2026 (IST). All dates are civil dates in India (IST).
Lunar festivals are computed from tithis and can differ by region and panchang. Where sources disagree, the item is marked **provisional** and both dates are listed. The app shows a "Date may vary by region" label on provisional items. Muhurat timings are deliberately left out of the product.

## User segments and use cases
(Qualitative; no demand volumes are claimed.)
- **Small businesses** (kirana, sweet shops, boutiques, salons, jewellers, cafes, D2C sellers on Instagram/WhatsApp): festival offer posts, WhatsApp broadcast text, product photo prompts, Google Business Profile posts. Rule: never invent discounts; offers come only from the user.
- **Creators and social media managers:** reel scripts, carousels, captions in Hinglish or regional languages, content calendars around the festival window.
- **Individuals and families:** greetings for parents, in-laws, colleagues and friends abroad; Karwa Chauth/Bhai Dooj personal messages; party or puja planning checklists.
- **Students and teachers:** respectful explainers (history, regional variations), school assembly speeches, essays. Rule: no invented rituals and no authoritative muhurat claims.
- **HR and corporate teams:** inclusive office greetings, Diwali gifting notes, event plans.
- **Designers:** image/poster prompts for Midjourney, Ideogram, ChatGPT images and Gemini, with a note that in-image text may render imperfectly, so text should be added later in Canva or Figma.

## Risks
- **Date errors:** mitigated by sources, a "may vary" label, the admin override table and lastVerified shown on each page.
- **Cultural/religious sensitivity:** no invented rituals, no muhurat claims, no mocking, no stereotypes; the prompts tell the AI to stay respectful and to flag uncertain traditions.
- **False marketing claims:** the prompts forbid inventing discounts, prices, stock, "limited time" or testimonials.
- **Vercel Hobby is non-commercial:** "the Hobby plan restricts users to non-commercial, personal use only" (https://vercel.com/docs/plans/hobby). Any monetisation needs a Pro plan or a different host. That's Raghav's decision; no plan change was made.
- **Image text rendering:** AI image tools may misspell text; every image prompt says to add text later.
- **Seasonality:** traffic is spiky around festivals, so the evergreen universal builder stays primary.
